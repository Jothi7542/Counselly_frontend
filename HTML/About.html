About Us

At Counselly, we believe that everyone deserves a safe space to talk, heal, and grow.


Our platform connects individuals with qualified psychologists and counsellors who provide professional mental health support in a confidential and compassionate environment.


Whether you’re struggling with stress, anxiety, relationship issues, or simply need someone to listen, Counselly is here to help. We make it easier to book sessions, choose your preferred language, and find the right psychologist who truly understands you.


Our mission is to break the stigma around mental health and make emotional well-being accessible to everyone—anytime, anywhere.

Mission and Vision

What we aim to be

We aim to educate that mental healthcare is beyond just therapy. Everyone deserves the best mental healthcare. Mental health is a universal human right.

Our goal is to extend our services throughout India, aiming for omnipresence across the country, and focusing on delivering quality mental wellness for everyone.
