Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

At Counselly, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, book a session, or contact us for support. This may include:

  • Name and contact details (email, phone number, address)
  • Demographic information (age, gender, preferred language)
  • Payment information (processed securely through our third-party provider)
  • Session details and history

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes, including:

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To facilitate session bookings and appointments
  • To process payments and prevent fraud
  • To improve our website and user experience
  • To communicate with you regarding your account or sessions

3. Data Confidentiality

Your mental health data and session discussions are strictly confidential. We do not share the content of your sessions with any third parties, except as required by law or in emergency situations where there is a risk of harm to yourself or others.

4. Information Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your profile settings or by contacting us directly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
